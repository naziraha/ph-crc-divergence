SETTINGS
  Epithelial: pan-epithelial structural candidates, used if max-min of stage medians <= 1 log2
    candidates missing from platform: none
  Stromal / immune: PanglaoDB human canonical markers (decoupler dc.op.resource);
    Stromal: Fibroblasts, Myofibroblasts, Smooth muscle cells, Endothelial cells, Pericytes
    Immune: T cells, B cells, Plasma cells, NK cells, Macrophages, Monocytes, Dendritic cells, Mast cells, Neutrophils
    genes in >1 compartment removed
  Genes per compartment: Epithelial 18, Stromal 292, Immune 447
  Epithelial genes used: EPCAM, CDH1, KRT8, KRT18, KRT19, CLDN3, CLDN4, ELF3, ESRP1, SPINT1, SPINT2, RAB25, GRHL2, OVOL1, PRSS8, ST14, CDS1, TJP3
  Strict epithelial set (sensitivity, range <= 0.75): 15 genes, drops GRHL2, CDS1, TJP3
  Scoring: decoupler ULM (tmin=5) on gene-centred log2 expression
  Organ signature: organ mean - max(normal colon, carcinoma mean) >= 3 log2, organ mean >= 8; metastases not used
  Organ fraction: carcinoma median + organ median mixture (linear scale), per-gene fraction,
    median across signature genes, clipped 0-1
  Adjustment: per gene OLS on all composition covariates; adjusted = residual + gene mean
